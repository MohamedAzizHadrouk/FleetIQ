Gestion du parc · extracted from the ChartDB model “FleetIQ DataBase” (PostgreSQL) · Fleet Management area only

1. Architecture overview

The Fleet Management area is organised in four layers. Customer is the tenant root: nearly every table carries a CustomerId. Car and the organisation tables (Driver, Department) are the core references that all operational domains point to.

2. Layers & responsibilities

Tenant

Customer (the company using FleetIQ, may be a reseller of other customers via ResellerId) and SalesCustomer (the customer's own clients). Every business row is scoped by CustomerId.

Core references

Car with its brand/model catalogue and installed telematics equipment; Driver, Department (tree) and Speciality describe who uses the vehicles.

Operational domains

Everything that happens to a vehicle: preventive maintenance & repairs, rentals with instalments, tyres and their mount/unmount log, missions and fuel cards, accidents and insurers.

Support

Administrative documents with validity/renewal log, and the stock & purchasing chain: supplier → commercial document → lines/payments, articles, recipes and stock movements.
